JINN PROJECT / PUBLIC WEB GAME

Source ofThought.

Choose works. Arrange ideas. Change the battle.

Literature, philosophy, science, history. Human works become cards in a 5 vs 5 battle where relationships and placement become strategy.

Play Source of Thought ↗Explore the game ↓

Play in your browser / Three.js · Web / Guest gameplay

WHAT IT IS

Play a
composition of ideas.

Collect works rather than heroes. Read each card's Family, Specialization, and Skill, then build a network of five. Your strategy is not just which cards are strong, but how they work together.

BUILD

Select.
Build. Position.

Choose works in Battle Preparation and inspect formation and Resonance before combat.

01 / DECK

10-card Deck

Build a ten-card deck by comparing Family, Specialization, stats, and Skills.

02 / MEMBERS

5 Battle Members

Select the five cards that will fight. Consider roles and combinations.

03 / FORMATION

5×5 Formation

Arrange front, back, and neighboring positions; inspect the Tactical Preview, then review your setup.

BATTLE

Relationships
in motion.

Watch a 5 vs 5 auto battle unfold through Skills, Status, and SP. See how your network fights—and when it needs an intervention.

Resonance

Neighboring works shape the network. Change placement and inspect the preview before battle.

Family

Mind → Human → System → Mind

Same-Family matchups and Karma / Neutral are neutral. Specializations do not have a matchup modifier.

Strategic SP

Read the battlefield and spend accumulated Strategic SP on a stratagem. It is a strategic resource distinct from Unit SP.

10 attributes / Specializations

Philosophy · Psychology · Science · Economy · Karma · Emotion · Morality · Ideology · Individual · Community

ATK / SATK / DEF / SDEF / SPD / ACC / EVA / HP / SP / LUCK. Read a card through its role and Skill, not color alone.

36 STRATAGEMS

Intervene.
Shift the battle.

Auto battle is not only a spectacle. Read a stratagem's effect and target, spend Strategic SP, and intervene in the ongoing battle. The Thirty-Six Stratagems form a distinct intervention layer alongside normal attacks and Skills.

IN-GAME

Victory / Result

Inspect the result, rematch with the same setup, or return to Preparation and rebuild. Preparation and battle form one continuous game loop.
